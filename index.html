Session Drummer

Play a part. Get a drummer.

Tap Record once, then play whenever you're ready. It starts recording on your first notes, without clipping them, and it stops by itself after about 3 seconds of quiet, or at 60 seconds. Then the mic turns off and your take stays put until you tap Record again.

Listening settings

If it starts on room noise or talking, pick "Loud notes". If it misses quiet fingerpicking, pick "Soft notes".
